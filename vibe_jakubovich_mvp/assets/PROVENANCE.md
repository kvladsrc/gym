# Art provenance

## Active 1990s studio assets

- `studio-90s.png`: empty velvet-and-incandescent TV stage, built-in OpenAI image
  generation, 2026-09-05. An artistic interpretation, not an exact reconstruction.
- `host-dance-alpha.png`: four full-body poses in one horizontal strip, built-in
  OpenAI generation using the original `studio.png` as an identity reference.
  A second built-in edit removed the draft's painted checkerboard and produced
  real RGBA transparency. Runtime sprite regions preserve that alpha unchanged.
- `host-dance.png`: first draft with a painted checkerboard; not used by the game
  and excluded from the export.
- `audio/studio.wav`, `audio/spin.wav`: original stereo arrangements composed and
  rendered by `tools/compose_music.gd`, not sampled TV music. No recordings or
  paid services. Rebuild with `just vibe_jakubovich_mvp music` inside Nix.

The exact new prompts are in [ART_PROMPTS.md](../docs/ART_PROMPTS.md).
Lights, letter flips, score animation and sparks are native Godot effects.

## Original MVP illustration (retained as reference)

`studio.png` was generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool
on 2026-09-05 for this game. It is an artistic interpretation of a TV studio
and Leonid Yakubovich, not a frame from a broadcast or a measured reconstruction.
The original output was copied from the generation directory into this project.
No image editing or background removal was applied.

The procedural wheel, UI and synthesized audio are generated by project code.
The Alegreya Sans SC Regular/Bold fonts are reused from
`cothic/assets/fonts/`; their upstream family is
[Alegreya Sans SC](https://github.com/huertatipografica/Alegreya-Sans).

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished background illustration for
a polished 2D Godot desktop game, nostalgic Russian word-guessing TV show
Pole Chudes. Create one wide 16:9 landscape image, ideally 1536x864 or larger.
Scene: a beautiful warmly lit 1990s television studio interpreted as premium
hand-painted realistic game art, deep midnight teal wall panels, walnut,
slender brass trims, theatrical amber practical lights, subtle cinematic haze
and burgundy stage curtains at far edges. Composition is critical: left 65
percent of the image is quiet dark teal studio wall in upper half and an empty
polished dark stage floor in lower half; reserve this area for actual game UI
and a separately rendered wheel, DO NOT paint a wheel, letter board, podium,
interface, lettering or symbols there. On the rightmost 30 percent, a warmly
recognizable Leonid Yakubovich, older Russian male television host with broad
friendly face, iconic thick white moustache, combed grey hair, expressive brows,
dressed in a beautifully textured dark midnight tuxedo, white shirt and black
bow tie, standing shown from head to upper thighs, holding a small dark cue
card with no markings at waist, other hand naturally welcoming the contestant.
Head near x=80%, y=24%, body stays in rightmost third. Anatomically natural
hands, charming restrained smile, dignified warm character, finely illustrated
facial detail. Theatrical warm key light from upper left, cool teal rim.
Sophisticated editorial painterly realism, not caricature, not plastic 3D,
not photomontage. Include beautiful layered architectural depth and subtle
floor reflections but keep left uncluttered and dark enough for ivory UI text.
No text, no logos, no watermark, no graphical overlay, no letters, no wheel.
This is final game art, not a mockup.
