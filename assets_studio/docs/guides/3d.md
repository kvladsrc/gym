# 3D: pipeline and prompts

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD013 -->

## Pipeline

**Qwen-Image → Hunyuan3D-2mini (`hunyuan3d`) → Hunyuan3D-Paint (`paint`).**
Paint uses the shape's source image and plausibly fills in unseen sides. The
UI's “Paint” button automatically selects the mesh and source image.

This choice followed a sweep of about 240 variants (FLUX and Qwen-Image ×
TripoSR, Stable Fast 3D and Hunyuan3D; cartoon, Gothic, realistic and painted
styles; heroes, enemies, props, artifacts, landscapes and entire levels):

| | TripoSR | Hunyuan3D-2mini |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing figure tilt | −4 … −17° | −0.9 … +1.3° |
| Leg gap (fraction of height) | 0.22–0.31 | 0.38–0.46 |
| Props | Tilted, need leveling | Stand upright |
| Unseen sides | Not invented | Plausibly invented |
| Time | Seconds | ~1 min shape + ~40 s paint |

Stable Fast 3D produces textures in a second but its shape quality is closer
to TripoSR. FLUX imposes its own style and ignores prohibitions (of four knights,
three raised their arms and two had explicitly forbidden capes).

### Images: Qwen-Image

- Qwen-Image clearly beats FLUX as an image generator (average 2.5 vs 1.6;
  on paired prompts Qwen won 10 of 21 cases, FLUX three).
- Paint turns Qwen's fine detail into mottled textures. Fix the prompt, not
  the model: **simple style, broad clean color areas** make Qwen-derived
  models better than FLUX-derived ones.
- 1024×1024 for props, 832×1216 for full-body figures; 40 steps; no transparency
  (Paint removes the background).

### Parameters

| Stage | Parameters |
| --- | --- |
| Hunyuan3D-2mini | 50 steps, guidance 5, octree 384; size-based `max_faces`: 12K log, 16K cart, 20K rock, 24K arch, 40K character |
| Paint | Texture 2048, `max_faces` matching shape (avoid >~60K: CPU unwrapping hundreds of thousands of triangles takes tens of minutes; the server reduces to its own `max_faces`) |

## Prompt: an object paragraph plus a shared suffix

The highest-rated prompts had two parts: a paragraph about the particular
object and a shared suffix for style, lighting, view and prohibitions. Example
suffix (substitute your world and palette):

> Single isolated environment game asset for *(place, world)*. Stylized
> hand-painted art matching *(neighboring assets)*, strong chunky silhouette,
> broad clean color areas, muted earthy palette, restrained sculpted
> details. Neutral matte diffuse studio lighting, no harsh cast shadows.
> Orthographic front three-quarter view at eye level. Entire object
> visible with generous margins, centered on plain white background. No
> surrounding terrain, no diorama, no text, no letters, no people, no
> floating parts, no thin fragile details.

What the paragraph does (from models rated 4–5):

1. **Sentences, not keyword lists.** Describe construction: “two steep faces
   visibly meeting at a broad rugged corner”, “a thick upper shelf projecting
   forward over a recessed lower face”. Comma-separated lists scored 1–3.
2. **One connected object.** “One cohesive…”, “a single coherent object”,
   “both wheels remain attached”: Hunyuan3D makes one watertight mesh; the
   image should not suggest detached pieces.
3. **State a stable base.** “Flat stable bottom”, “flat underside”: the model
   can stand on terrain without sinking or tipping.
4. **Counts and proportions.** “Two broad strata, three large fractured
   planes”, “two thick piers, a single broad arch”: counted structures rather
   than scattered elements.
5. **Express game requirements as visible facts.** For a passage: “the opening
   is completely empty, wide enough for two people side by side, plain white
   background visible through it all the way down to the ground; no door,
   no bars, no floor slab across it”. Seeing the background through it makes
   the opening real in 3D.
6. **Object-specific prohibitions.** Cart: “no debris field, no barrels, no
   horse, no thin ropes”; log: “slightly hollow but not a pipe, no root fan”.
   Keep general prohibitions in the suffix.
7. **A view that reveals shape.** “Three-quarter view clearly showing both
   rock faces”, “side-front view showing the wheels and the open bed”:
   Hunyuan3D infers unseen sides from visible ones.
8. **Two or three colors from a shared palette.** The set reads as one place,
   and large solid regions paint cleanly: blue-grey slate, muted ochre lichen,
   olive moss. A palette without variation (“moss greens and cold greys”)
   produced a nearly monochrome green world; assign colors per object.
9. **Matte lighting without shadows.** Otherwise shadows get baked into color
   and Paint struggles with them.
10. **Tie it to a place.** “For a dark forest sanctuary… matching ancient
    woodland ruins”, rather than a bare style keyword.

## What works poorly

- **Voids, fine gaps and cavities.** Arched roots with gaps beneath, or a
  sarcophagus defined by a dark opening under a shifted lid: Hunyuan3D fills
  them or tears the surface. Large openings work only if the background is visible.
- **Thin and porous structures.** Dense foliage, bushes, thin bars and branches
  become blobs. Better: “a few large rounded clumps”, “thick stone posts with
  iron bars”, dead trees with thick blunt branches.
- **Top-down views.** A ground tile becomes an upright plate (the image is
  interpreted as the shape's front). Use about 30° and “a thin slab of earth”.
- **An entire level as a diorama.** Output is diorama-scale with centimeter
  details, unsuitable for third-person navigation (average level rating 1.3
  vs 3.9 for enemies). Build levels from separate objects; terrain from tiles
  or in an editor.
- **Composite objects in one phrase.** “A plank table on two barrels” became
  one barrel. Describe construction (rule 1).
- **Sky.** Qwen's equirectangular panoramas include ground in the lower half
  despite “no ground”. The horizon is central; terrain must hide the bottom.
  Studio has no panorama/skybox mode.

## Background: lost tree crowns and sword blades (fixed)

`hunyuan3d` and `paint` remove opaque-image backgrounds themselves (rembg).
Default rembg (`u2net`) removed **entire tree crowns** (silver, crimson and
green) and **silver sword blades**. Hunyuan3D received trunks with roots or
hilts without blades, which no prompt could fix. With `isnet-general-use`
(now in both servers, ~1 second per image), the same concepts produced complete
crowns and blades.

- Pale objects on white (silver foliage, white stone, steel) remain vulnerable.
  If a 3D silhouette loses a part, check the cutout first.
- Paint can shift hues (a crimson crown became purple).

## Concept failures (results rated 0)

- **“Reference sheet”, “character sheet”, “turnaround”** produce multiple views,
  labels and detail panels. For 3D use one figure: “full body single…”.
- **FLUX and SDXL ignore prohibitions:** capes, tabards, held swords and raised
  arms despite “no cape, empty hands”. Qwen-Image follows them.
- **Vague prompts** (“3D character design, cartoon style, standing pose”) yield
  generic characters unrelated to the game's world. Specify identity, clothes
  and setting.
- **“Low-poly”, “in the style of a 2001 game”** yield grey/dark backgrounds and
  thin branches; image polygons do not become mesh polygons.
- **Level dioramas:** Hunyuan3D reads 45° isometric views as fronts, turning the
  top into a front wall and losing the play area. Paint colors dark Gothic
  concepts almost black.
- **Thin flat objects viewed end-on** (a sword blade from the front) are easily
  lost in background removal and mesh simplification.

## Detail scale

Image details suit the depicted object's size. A ground tile stretched to
18 m turns pebbles into boulders; flattening height to 30% gives softer terrain.
Specify details for the actual intended size.

## Trees: deep roots, not flat bases

A tree with a flat base (“standing on its own roots”, “flat base”) exposes it
on any slope. Instead extend the trunk below ground into a deep tapering mass
of several thick roots (about a third of trunk height), as though uprooted.
It can then be sunk into any terrain shape. Prompt: “no flat base, no ground
disc, no soil”. The `trees1` sweep is the first test of Hunyuan3D's deep roots;
add its findings here.
